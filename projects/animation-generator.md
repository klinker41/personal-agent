# Project: Animation-Generator

## Storage & Architecture

- Stored on `unraid-nas` at `/mnt/user/ai/animation-generator` and bind-mounted
  into the Antigravity container (`stacks/unraid-nas/ai/compose.yaml`) at
  `/workspace/animation-generator`.
- Pluggable multi-style architecture: style-agnostic pipeline logic (rendering,
  audio, subtitles, stitching, kinematics in `scripts/kinematics.ts`, auditing)
  and shared static TTF fonts (`resources/fonts/`: `Outfit` 400/600/700/800 and
  `Cinzel` 400/700, replacing variable fonts for readable popups) live at the
  repo root, while style-specific primitives and visual rules live under
  `.agents/skills/`.
- Assets are programmatic TypeScript SVG modules rasterized via
  `@resvg/resvg-js` (bundling `Outfit` and `Cinzel`), rather than generated by
  an image model.
- Projects under `projects/<slug>/` organize multi-scene adaptations with
  `project.json`, `chapters/` (chapter-based projects), `voices.json`,
  `soundtrack.json`, `assets/characters/`, per-scene `scenes/<slug>/`
  (`script.json`, `scene.ts`, `qa_sheet.png`, `qa_proxy.mp4`, `scene.mkv`), and
  a stitched `output/movie.mkv` master with stem-mixed audio and embedded
  `.srt` subtitles.

## Art-Style Skills & Rigging Primitives

- **`cel-animation`** (`.agents/skills/cel-animation/`, preserved with shims
  and tests for Harry Potter movies such as `philosophers-stone` and
  `chamber-of-secrets`): enforces bold ink outlines, flat fills, stepped cel
  shadows, two-pass `<rect>` capsule limbs, and standardized model sheets
  (`front`/`three_quarter`/`side` views, 5 visemes, blink states) via
  `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/skills/cel-animation/SKILL.md`. Provides concentric
  two-pass rigging primitives (`renderTaperedCapsulePath`, `renderCelArm`,
  `renderCelLeg`, `renderCelNeck`, `renderCharacterModelSheet`) in
  `scripts/rigging.ts` (re-exported via `scripts/common.ts`) alongside shims in
  `.agents/skills/cel-animation/scripts/` (`render_asset.ts`,
  `render_scene.ts`, `common.ts`).
- **`graphic-novel-animation`** (`.agents/skills/graphic-novel-animation/`):
  full-color, scene-driven adult comic style with sculpted cubic-Bézier anatomy
  in `primitives.ts`, 2-point perspective staging, ink hatching, and
  scene-specific color scripts (not noir-only). Includes `buildBarefootPath`
  merged into `renderGraphicLeg` (no ankle seam lines on barefoot rigs) and
  enlarged `renderSystemHudPanel` panels/typography.
- **Graphic-novel narrative & staging defaults** (`SKILL.md`): slower-paced
  60–110s scenes (~120–220 spoken words, 6–12+ beats including
  reaction/breathing beats), 5–10s multi-shot cuts (no static wide shots >10s),
  dense foreground/midground/background set detail, `angle: 'side'` rigs for
  horizontal walks, signed `worldX` stride calculation, and locomotion
  telemetry (`worldX`, `facing`, `walkPhase`, `walkAmount`, `stepSpanPx`) in
  `FrameOutput.actors`.
- **Shared humanoid arm/hand primitives** (verified via `bun test`):
  implemented in `.agents/skills/graphic-novel-animation/primitives.ts`
  (`renderGraphicArm`, `renderSculptedForearmPath`, `buildGraphicHand`) and
  `.agents/skills/cel-animation/primitives.ts` (`renderCelArm`,
  `renderCelForearmPath`, `buildCelHand`). Both use flush forearm wrist
  terminations with anatomical bare-forearm tapering (`bareForearm` flag on cel
  arms), open-top Pass 1 ink contours with tucked stroke-free Pass 2 fills,
  wrist-coupled hand sizing (`wWrist`, `palmScale`), front-view right-arm thumb
  mirroring (`thumbDir`), bare-wrist shadow continuity on graphic-novel rigs,
  and sculpted Bézier silhouettes for all five `HandPose` states (`relaxed`,
  `fist`, `point`, `open_palm`, `cup`).

## Audio Mixing & Kinematics

- **Audio mixing** (`.agents/skills/cel-animation/scripts/audio_mix.ts`:
  `renderSpanWav`, `combineWavTracks`, `mixSceneAudio`, `mixMovieAudio`; tested
  in `tests/pipeline.test.ts` via `bun test`): `mixMovieAudio` renders audio
  spans at natural duration rather than pre-padding temp WAVs to full movie
  length, positions tracks in-memory via `combineWavTracks`
  (`PositionedWavTrack` with FFmpeg `adelay` + `apad`/`atrim`) across dialogue,
  ambience, music, and SFX buses, and deletes per-span temp files after each
  stem master (`master_dialogue.wav`, `master_ambience.wav`,
  `master_music.wav`, `master_sfx.wav`) completes, preventing quadratic
  `O(N^2)` temp disk growth on multi-scene renders.
- **Walk-cycle kinematics** (`scripts/kinematics.ts`, `scripts/audit.ts`,
  `scripts/audit_project.ts`): `computeStepSpanPx` and signed `worldX` in
  `computeStridePhase` drive toe-off knee flex and heel-strike cushioning,
  while `auditSceneWalkCycles` automatically flags moonwalking, rigid sliding
  (`isGroundedNonWalk`), `walkPhase` speed snaps, and walk-in-place before
  visual QA.

## QA Gates & Programmatic Auditing

- Shared `scene-audit` skill (`.agents/skills/scene-audit/SKILL.md`, used by
  both art styles) structures QA into three gates:
  - **Gate 1 (Assets)**: visual asset and stride-cycle checks, plus
    `auditPoseConnectivity` in `scripts/render_asset.ts` (pixel flood-fill +
    `1px` erosion) enforcing single-component silhouettes and rejecting
    detached limbs, floating hands/heads, or `<3px` pinched joints across all
    poses.
  - **Gate 2 (Scenes)**: `qa_sheet.png` and `qa_proxy.mp4` visual/audio audit
    checking moonwalking, backward sliding, stiff-leg skating, facing-vs-travel
    consistency, `>12s` static holds, and downscaled HUD legibility.
  - **Gate 3 (Publish)**: stitched master `output/movie.mkv` audit.
- Visual/audio inspection delegates to a fresh read-only `research` subagent on
  `flash` given only rendered outputs (`*_sheet.png`, `qa_sheet.png`,
  `qa_proxy.mp4`) and `script.json`/`soundtrack.json`, enforcing a
  `PASS`/`FAIL` fix-and-re-render loop (complementary to `self-review-commit`,
  which audits git diffs via a `self` subagent).
- Programmatic pre-review CLI/library `scripts/audit_project.ts` (tested in
  `tests/audit-project.test.ts`):
  - Validates `script.json` references against `voices.json`,
    `soundtrack.json`, beat IDs, `dissolve` lead-in/lead-out overlaps, and
    `audio_synth.py`-compatible audio specs (`music: false`, inline music
    `prompt`, stinger SFX `{ type: "stinger", prompt }`,
    `ambience: "synth:<name>"`, library paths, and object ambience forms).
  - Probes `scene.mkv` and `output/movie.mkv` streams, subtitles, and
    durations; verifies `qa_sheet.png`, `qa_proxy.mp4`, and
    `sound_manifest.json` stem paths on disk; uses FFmpeg `volumedetect`
    (`parseVolumedetect`, `measureAudioVolume`) to flag silent tracks,
    clipping, resolution mismatches, stale `movie.mkv`, and `>6 dB`
    scene-to-scene loudness jumps.

## Dungeon Crawler Carl (DCC) Series

- Per-floor 4K `graphic-novel-animation` movie series under
  `projects/dcc-floor-*` (sorted numerically by starting floor, separate from
  non-DCC projects), covering Floors 1–9 through *This Inevitable Ruin*:
  `dcc-floor-1` (renamed from `projects/dungeon-crawler-carl/`), `dcc-floor-2`,
  `dcc-floor-3`, `dcc-floor-4`, `dcc-floor-5`, `dcc-floor-6-7` (combining
  short Floors 6–7), `dcc-floor-8`, and `dcc-floor-9`.
- **Uniform floor layout** (targeting 60–120 min movies): machine-readable
  `parts.json` manifest for incremental part-by-part generation (conserving
  quota vs. the 1-chapter-per-part Harry Potter pattern) plus LLM-ingestible
  blueprints (`proposal/overview.md`, `proposal/part-XX.md`).
- **`dcc-floor-1` specifics**: 11 sequential multi-scene parts (60 scenes,
  Book 1 Ch. 1–27, 3840x2160 HEVC `output/movie.mkv`), initial assets
  (`carl.ts`, `princess-donut.ts`, `seattle-collapse.ts`,
  `title-card-stage.ts`), and opening scenes (`01-the-collapse`,
  `02-title-card`). Its `proposal/` blueprints (`overview.md`,
  `part-01.md`–`part-11.md`) specify show-don't-tell visuals, selective Carl
  V.O., comedic System AI/HUD panels, Floor 1 vs. Floor 2 canon boundaries,
  biome/gear/voice/soundtrack continuity registries, and paraphrased R-rated
  dialogue to avoid LLM copyright recitation blocks.
- **Daily `dcc-movie-generator` sidecar pipeline**: advances the active floor
  by one part per run via `parts.json` (auto-advancing floors upon completion),
  configures `voices.json` and `soundtrack.json`, renders 4K scenes, stitches
  cumulative 4K `output/movie.mkv`, validates all three `scene-audit` QA gates,
  runs `bun test` and `self-review-commit`, pushes to `main`, and notifies
  Slack.
- **DCC tests**: `tests/dcc-floor-1.test.ts` (renamed from
  `tests/dungeon-crawler-carl.test.ts`), `tests/dcc-proposals.test.ts` (Floors
  2–9 structure/continuity/formatting via `bun test`), and
  `test_dcc_movie_generator.py`.
- **User visual & canon preferences (`dcc-floor-*`)**:
  - Omit artificial neon blue rim stripes on Carl and Princess Donut.
  - Carl: worn dark leather jacket bare-chested, heart boxers, and toggleable
    `pink_crocs` / `left_croc_only` / `barefoot` footwear.
  - Princess Donut: plush tortoiseshell Persian fur silhouette.
  - Seattle collapse: depict all buildings destroyed (no standing buildings).
