# Knowledge: Antigravity-Mermaid-Rendering

- Antigravity artifact renderer supports flowchart/graph, stateDiagram-v2,
sequenceDiagram, classDiagram, erDiagram, and xychart-beta Mermaid types, but
does not support gantt charts.
